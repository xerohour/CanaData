## 2024-06-20 - Accessible Product Images
**Learning:** Adding `aria-label` to the link wrapper and `alt` to the image improves screen reader clarity, particularly when the link opens a lightbox plugin like fancybox. The product name was used to make the labels context-aware.
**Action:** When creating image thumbnails that link to lightboxes, ensure the link describes the action ("View high-resolution image of [Product]") and the image has a descriptive alt text ("Thumbnail of [Product]").
