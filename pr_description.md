💡 What: Added `aria-hidden="true"` span to the decorative emoji icon (✨) in `generate_report.py`, and added semantic meaning (`role="img"` and `aria-label`) to the inline noun emojis (❤️ and ☕) in `parse-script/CanaParse.py`.
🎯 Why: Without `aria-hidden="true"`, screen readers will announce decorative emoji strings, causing a confusing user experience. Furthermore, the inline noun emojis in the footer are not purely decorative; they replace the words "love" and "coffee". By providing them with semantic meaning via `role="img"` and `aria-label`, we ensure the screen reader correctly announces the sentence structure.
📸 Before/After: Before, screen readers might read out "Sparkles PROMO:" or just the default description of the heart and coffee cup. Now, they will cleanly read "PROMO:" and "Generated with love and coffee".
♿ Accessibility: Improves screen reader navigation and clarity by hiding decorative emojis that add no semantic meaning, while providing proper semantic labels for inline noun emojis.
